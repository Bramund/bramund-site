Technical “Partners”

One of our greatest strengths is the companies we represent. They develop cutting edge technology and trust our technical ability to take their names and know-how to all foundries in Latin America.

  • Engineering, design, and manufacture of foundry toolings and molds. Working with sand, HPDC, low pressure and gravity process.

  • Specialists in the Cold Box process. Manufacturer of core shooters, sand mixers, gassing systems and more. Provides turn-key projects for core rooms and molding lines.

  • Specialists in no-bake molding systems. Mixers with one or two arms, fast-loops, roll-over, shake-out, mechanical, and thermal sand regenerators and more…

  • Specialists in turn-key solutions for metal quality control, since the charge process until the pouring lines, and in intelligent and autonomous coating systems, allowing automatic density controls and extremely homogenous mixing.

  • Specialists in finishing foundry solutions. Robotic deburring cells, decoring machines, trimming machines, cooling systems, and others.

  • The most innovative manufacturer of hydraulic manipulators to foundries and forges. They also provide Hummers, Circuit Breakers, Carriage Technologies, grinding manipulators and more…

  • Experts in greenfield foundry projects, with focus on sand preparation plants, charging cars and dust collector systems.

  • Innovative manufacturer of sand laboratory equipment for the correct monitoring and control of all types of sand processes.

  • A global leader in binder jet 3D printing of metal, sand, and ceramics, ExOne provides industrial 3D printing systems and services.

  • Major Latin-American manufacturer of Induction Furnaces. With a service factory in México, count with original spare parts stock and a highly skilled technical team to assist your company.

  • Company by the Kuttner group, specialized in technical solutions for cupola furnaces.

  • Specialist in the design and manufacture of fixtures for machining process.

  • Specialists in the manufacture of manual equipment for the finishing area. Wheel grinder, swing frame, Sanding belt machine and others.
